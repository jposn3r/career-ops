# Story Bank -- Master STAR+R Stories

This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file -- your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" -> combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
   - "Tell me about your most impactful project" -> pick your highest-impact story
   - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" -> find a story with a Reflection

## Stories

### [Leadership Growth] IC to Director at fuboTV
**Source:** Report #001 -- Netflix -- EM TV Product UI
**S:** Joined fuboTV as IC building Roku features. Company scaling rapidly with no org structure for TV platforms.
**T:** Needed to build and structure an engineering org to ship across Roku, SmartTV, Apple TV, mobile, web.
**A:** Rebuilt eng org in partnership with senior leadership, transitioned to cross-functional pod model, grew from IC to supporting 20+ direct reports (ICs + managers).
**R:** Delivered multi-platform Home page, profile selection, live sports stats across all platforms.
**Reflection:** Org design is never done -- I'd now be more intentional about cross-pod communication rituals from day one.
**Best for questions about:** leadership growth, org design, change management, scaling teams, biggest challenge

### [Change Management] AI Tooling Adoption at Meta
**Source:** Report #001 -- Netflix -- EM TV Product UI
**S:** Engineering org at Meta RL was not using AI tools; skepticism about AI-assisted development.
**T:** Needed to demonstrate value without mandating adoption, changing engineering culture bottom-up.
**A:** Championed Claude Code personally, built custom workflows, showed measurable wins in code review quality and dev velocity. Led by example, not by decree.
**R:** Team and cross-functional partners adopted AI tooling; became a reference for other Meta orgs.
**Reflection:** Change management works best when you lead by example and show results, not when you mandate process.
**Best for questions about:** driving change, innovation, leading without authority, culture change, new technology adoption

### [Cross-Functional] Quest 3 Launch Coordination
**Source:** Report #001 -- Netflix -- EM TV Product UI
**S:** Navigator delivery for Quest 3 required coordinating across 20+ partner teams with a hard launch deadline.
**T:** Needed to align priorities, resolve conflicts, and ensure OS-level features shipped on time.
**A:** Defined product strategy, ran cross-functional prioritization, managed tradeoffs. Features went through Zuckerberg review.
**R:** Quest 3 launched successfully with Navigator features intact, on time.
**Reflection:** With 20+ stakeholders, the informed captain model (one DRI per decision) is essential -- consensus doesn't scale.
**Best for questions about:** cross-functional leadership, stakeholder management, competing priorities, executive visibility

### [0-to-1 Product] Quest OS Navigation Redesign
**Source:** Report #001 -- Netflix -- EM TV Product UI
**S:** Quest OS navigation needed a fundamental redesign to match the UX bar set by Apple Vision Pro.
**T:** Conceived, prototyped, and drove a 0-to-1 redesign of the navigation experience.
**A:** Built prototypes personally to validate direction, collaborated closely with design, iterated based on user testing, shipped through executive review.
**R:** Delivered refreshed navigation experience as a flagship OS feature.
**Reflection:** Prototyping as an EM is not about writing production code -- it's about de-risking the roadmap and building conviction.
**Best for questions about:** 0-to-1 development, product vision, UX leadership, prototyping, executive alignment

### [Technical Excellence] fuboTV Release Cadence + Crash Rate
**Source:** Report #001 -- Netflix -- EM TV Product UI
**S:** Roku releases shipping every 5 weeks; crash rate was 13%. Too slow for a live TV product.
**T:** Transform release engineering without breaking stability.
**A:** Restructured CI/CD pipeline, introduced automated testing gates, worked with QA on regression strategy.
**R:** Improved release cadence from 5 weeks to weekly. Reduced crash rate from 13% to 0.1%.
**Reflection:** Speed and stability aren't tradeoffs -- better automation gets you both. The crash rate improvement was a side effect of faster feedback loops.
**Best for questions about:** technical challenges, quality improvement, process improvement, biggest impact

### [Data-Driven] fuboTV KPI/OKR Framework
**Source:** Report #001 -- Netflix -- EM TV Product UI
**S:** fuboTV lacked formalized metrics across the technology org.
**T:** Co-authored product roadmap with CPO and VP of Product; needed to establish KPIs/OKRs.
**A:** Set up OKR framework across engineering, partnered with Product, BizDev, Finance, Marketing to align.
**R:** Organization moved from intuition-based to data-driven decision making.
**Reflection:** OKRs work when used for alignment, not performance management. Keeping them separate from reviews was key.
**Best for questions about:** metrics, data-driven decisions, aligning engineering with business, measuring success

### [People Management] Letting Someone Go
**Source:** Report #001 -- Netflix -- EM TV Product UI
**S:** Had an engineer at fuboTV who was technically capable but consistently missed deadlines and created friction.
**T:** Address performance directly -- coaching hadn't moved the needle after 2 quarters.
**A:** Had direct conversations, set clear expectations with timeline, documented gaps, made the decision to part ways.
**R:** Team velocity improved and morale lifted; remaining engineers felt their standards were respected.
**Reflection:** The hardest part isn't the decision -- it's the delay. I learned to act sooner. Keeping underperformers hurts the team more than the discomfort of the conversation.
**Best for questions about:** firing, low performers, difficult conversations, keeper test, team health

### [Platform Strategy] International Expansion at fuboTV
**Source:** Report #001 -- Netflix -- EM TV Product UI
**S:** fuboTV needed to expand into international markets (Spain, Canada) on a US-only platform.
**T:** Expand platform coverage while maintaining stability on existing devices.
**A:** Led platform engineering to support internationalization (localization, content rights, regional device variations).
**R:** Successfully launched in Spain and Canada, expanding the addressable market.
**Reflection:** International expansion touches content, legal, device certification, and support. Cross-functional alignment matters more than technical complexity.
**Best for questions about:** navigating ambiguity, platform decisions, growing into new areas, strategic thinking
